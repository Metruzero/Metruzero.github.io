Tutoring

I have a life long love of mathematics and computer science. One thing I've always heard from both children and adults is that they have a strong dislike for math. This is something that I've always found disapointing as I always loved the subject.

My tutoring is focused on increasing engagement and enthusiasm for the subject in order to set up the student for life long success. There are a lot of things to enjoy about learning and mathematics and my goal is to share that with the people I teach.

Pricing

I offer free consultation for all new students, this is an opportunity for you to ask me questions about my style of teaching and for me to ask you questions about the student so that I can help prepare them for the most success.

After the consultation, I charge on a per session basis of fifty dollars per session. This pricing includes lesson prep before the lesson, the lesson itself and a follow up email or call afterwards of their progress.
